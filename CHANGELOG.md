## 1.0.0

- Initial stable release of `adaptive_context_menu`.
- 3D pop preview elevation with real-time backdrop Gaussian blur.
- Intelligent collision positioning engine: auto-detects screen bounds and positions menu above/below target to avoid off-screen clipping.
- Platform-adaptive styling: Cupertino frosted glass on iOS/macOS and Material 3 tonal elevation on Android/others.
- Full desktop & web support with secondary click (right-click) and mobile long-press detection.
- Customizable actions, dividers, leading icons, subtitles, and destructive action themes.
- Haptic feedback on menu presentation and selection clicks.
- 100% test coverage across widget lifecycles.
